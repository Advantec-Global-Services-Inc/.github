# Governance

This document describes how projects in the Advantec Global Services GitHub organization are managed. Individual repositories may define their own governance, which takes precedence.

## Overview

Repositories in this organization are stewarded by Advantec Global Services, Inc. Our engineering team maintains them, sets technical direction, and makes final decisions on scope and releases. We welcome outside contributions within that structure — see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Roles

- **Maintainers** — Advantec engineers with write access. They review and merge pull requests, triage issues, cut releases, and are accountable for the health of the project.
- **Contributors** — anyone who submits issues, pull requests, or participates in discussions. Sustained, quality contributions may lead to an invitation to become a maintainer, at the maintainers' discretion.

## Decision making

- Day-to-day decisions (bug fixes, small improvements) are made by maintainers through normal review.
- Significant changes — new features, breaking changes, architectural shifts — should start as an issue describing the proposal, so direction is agreed before implementation.
- When consensus is not reached, Advantec engineering leadership makes the final call.

## Releases

Maintainers decide release timing and versioning. Where a repository follows a versioning scheme (such as semantic versioning), it is documented in that repository.

## Changes to governance

Changes to this document are made by Advantec engineering leadership. Material changes will be noted in the commit history of this repository.
