# Support

Thanks for using an Advantec Global Services project. Here is how to get help.

## Open source projects

- **Bugs and feature requests** — open an issue on the affected repository. Search existing issues first; add your details to an existing issue rather than opening a duplicate.
- **Questions** — open an issue, or use the repository's Discussions tab where enabled.
- **Security issues** — never open a public issue. Follow our [security policy](SECURITY.md) instead.

When reporting a problem, include the version or commit you are running, your environment, what you expected, and what happened instead. The faster we can reproduce it, the faster we can fix it.

## Advantec clients

If you are an Advantec client, issues here are for the open source projects only. For anything related to your engagement — managed services, cloud, networking, or end user computing — contact your Advantec engagement lead or reach us through [www.advantec.us](https://www.advantec.us).

## Response expectations

These repositories are maintained by our engineering team alongside client work. We triage new issues regularly, but response times vary with workload. Well-written reports with reproduction steps get attention first.
